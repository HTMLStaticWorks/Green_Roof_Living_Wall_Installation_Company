Technical Exploded Anatomy

Engineered cross-section of a living system.

A living roof or wall is an intricate multi-layer engineering assembly. Each stratum performs a vital biochemical and structural function.

Layer 01

Vegetative Canopy

Specially cultivated sedums, perennial grasses, and vertical climbing flora selected for localized microclimate and high drought resistance.

Layer 02

Engineered Substrate

Non-compacting mineral-based engineered soil with expanded clay, pumice, and organic amendments tailored to specific load specifications.

Layer 03

Filter Geotextile Fleece

Water-permeable needle-punched fabric preventing fine media migration while keeping drainage channels completely unobstructed.

Layer 04

Drainage & Retention Core

High-impact polystyrene dimpled core that stores critical gallons of rainwater for plant roots while channeling surplus storm water to drains.

Layer 05

Root Permeation Barrier

FLL-tested heavy polyolefin sheet preventing aggressive botanical root systems from penetrating structural waterproofing layers.

Layer 06

Waterproofing & Deck

Continuous heat-welded monolithic elastomeric membrane directly adhered to structural concrete, steel, or timber with electronic leak monitoring.
