Structural Ecology

Green Roof Systems

Transforming dead roof surfaces into performing ecological assets. We design systems precisely tailored to building load capacity, stormwater mandates, and aesthetic goals.

Extensive Sedum Systems

15–30 lbs/sq ft

Lightweight, low-profile assembly featuring shallow engineered growing media (3–6 inches). Ideal for retrofit commercial roofs and large industrial spans requiring stormwater management and thermal buffering with minimal structural reinforcement.

• Drought-hardy Sedums • Low maintenance cycle • 70%+ Stormwater capture

Intensive Rooftop Gardens

60–150+ lbs/sq ft

Accessible, park-like amenity spaces requiring deeper engineered substrate (8–36+ inches). Supports mature trees, perennial flowering shrubs, water features, and pedestrian decking for high-value residential and corporate developments.

• Full tenant amenity • Deep botanical diversity • Sub-surface smart irrigation

Biosolar & Retention Roofs

Dual Synergy

Combines photovoltaic solar arrays with living roof vegetation. The vegetative microclimate keeps solar panels at optimal operating temperatures, increasing energy yield by up to 12% while providing storm surge retention.

• +12% Solar efficiency • Zero roof penetrations • Peak runoff attenuation

Exterior Architectural Facades

Modular Cassettes

Engineered to endure hurricane-force wind shear, severe thermal fluctuations, and harsh direct sunlight. Our exterior cladding panels feature marine-grade aluminum substructures with integrated drainage troughs and thermal breaks.

• Wind tunnel certified up to 130 mph • Non-combustible A1-rated framing • Facade heat reduction (-12°C)

Interior Bio-Wall Atriums

Phytoremediation

Tailored for corporate lobbies, hospitals, and central atriums. These hydroponic or light-substrate walls act as living air filters, absorbing VOCs, carbon dioxide, and fine particulate matter while delivering sound dampening across busy interiors.

• Automated nutrient dosing • Full spectrum circadian grow lights • Acoustic NRC rating 0.75+

Closed-Loop Smart Fertigation

IoT Telemetry

Zero water waste through recirculating subterranean tanks. Smart multi-zone manifolds monitor soil moisture, pH, and electrical conductivity 24/7, sending real-time telemetry to our engineering team.

• 90% Water reduction vs manual • Cellular telemetry sensor alarms • Anti-clog pressure-compensating emitters

Zero-Leakage Assurance

Structural Waterproofing & Membrane Protection

A living roof is only as good as the waterproofing beneath it. Our certified roofing engineers specialize in seamless hot-air welded membranes, chemical-free root barriers, and electronic leak verification.

1

EFVM Electronic Leak Testing

High-voltage vector mapping pinpoints pinhole flaws with 100% precision prior to substrate overburden placement.

2

FLL-Certified Root Barriers

High-density polyethylene shields impervious to root rhizomes, ensuring the structural slab remains untouched forever.

3

25-Year Commercial Warranty

Single-source manufacturer and installer warranty covering membrane integrity and plant health simultaneously.

Single-Ply Elastomeric Membrane Seaming
Certified robotic and manual hot-air welding on parapets and drainage wells to guarantee a hermetic seal.
